Firmware · ESP32-S3

Handheld Barcode Terminal

FreeRTOS firmware that enumerates a commercial USB barcode scanner through the ESP-IDF USB Host HID stack as a boot-protocol keyboard, translating HID scancodes to ASCII and assembling complete barcode strings.

The application is partitioned into core-pinned FreeRTOS tasks — USB host, HID event queue, display and battery, debounced input — sharing a mutex-guarded SSD1306 OLED that reports battery level, a four-position channel selector, the scan count and the decoded barcode. Li-ion monitoring uses a ten-sample averaged ADC reading across a 2:1 divider mapped over the 3.0–4.2 V range.

Hardware · Altium Designer

RFID Reader Board

An SMD board designed around an AOD4185 P-channel load switch, BC817 drivers, an 18 V zener transient clamp, a buzzer and a four-button interface — including custom schematic symbols and footprints drawn from manufacturer datasheets.

Delivered with a sourced bill of materials carrying manufacturer part numbers and distributor pricing, so the board could be quoted and ordered without a second sourcing pass.

Firmware · STM32G0

Ultrasonic Ranging Module

HC-SR04 ranging on an STM32G030K6 (Cortex-M0+) with a 1 MHz TIM1 time base for microsecond echo timing and 16-bit counter overflow handling.

Timeout guards fail safe by returning a negative distance when no echo comes back, rather than blocking in a wait loop or reporting a stale value — the difference between a sensor that reports nothing and a robot that stops responding.
