Selected work

Handheld barcode terminal

FreeRTOS firmware on an ESP32-S3 that talks to a commercial USB barcode scanner through the ESP-IDF USB Host HID stack, receiving keyboard reports and assembling them into complete barcode strings.

The application is split into core pinned tasks for USB host communication, HID event handling, display and battery management, and user input. Shared access to the SSD1306 OLED is protected by a mutex, which is where the concurrency actually bites.

Battery voltage is read through the ESP32-S3 ADC with averaging for stability, across a divider that maps the Li-ion range onto the input. I also designed the carrier PCB in Altium.

RFID reader board

An SMD interface and control board built around an AOD4185 P channel load switch, BC817 transistor drivers and an 18 V zener transient clamp, with a buzzer, a four button interface and the antenna and clock/data interfaces to the reader module.

Custom schematic symbols and footprints were drawn from manufacturer datasheets. Delivered with a fully sourced bill of materials carrying manufacturer part numbers and distributor pricing, plus the serial capture firmware used during bring up.

Ultrasonic ranging module

HC-SR04 ranging on an STM32G030K6 with a 1 MHz TIM1 time base for microsecond echo timing and 16 bit counter overflow handling.

Timeout guards fail safe by returning a negative distance when no echo comes back, rather than blocking in a wait loop or reporting a stale value. That is the difference between a sensor that reports nothing and a robot that stops responding.
