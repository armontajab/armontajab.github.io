Power System Analysis I, autumn 2025

Transmission line parameter calculator

A MATLAB tool that computes per-kilometre inductance and capacitance for transposed three-phase transmission lines with arbitrary conductor bundle geometry.

MATLABGMD / GMRbundle conductorstransposed linespower systems

What it computes

The inductance and capacitance of a three-phase line depend on the geometry of the conductors: how far apart the phases sit, and how the individual conductors within each bundle are arranged. The tool takes that geometry as input and returns the per-kilometre line parameters.

Method

For a transposed line, each phase occupies every position over the length of the line, so the asymmetry averages out and the three phases can be characterised by a single equivalent spacing. The calculation uses the geometric mean distance between phases together with the bundle-equivalent geometric mean radius, which collapses a bundle of several sub-conductors into one effective conductor. Handling arbitrary bundle geometry meant computing that equivalent radius from the sub-conductor positions rather than relying on the standard two- and four-conductor formulas.

What I took from it

Writing the calculation as a general tool rather than solving one configuration by hand forced me to understand which parts of the standard formulas are actually general and which are special cases of a particular bundle arrangement.
