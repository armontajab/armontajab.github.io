What I work on

My work sits where digital logic meets physical hardware. At Tensor Intelligent Machines I write firmware for handheld and sensing subsystems on autonomous parcel sorting robots, design the boards they run on, and repair the ones that come back from the field.

Outside that, the through line is closed loop systems: read a sensor, decide, drive an actuator, stay stable. A steel ball held in mid air by a camera and a PID loop. An STM32 regulating water level and temperature at the same time. An 8 bit datapath wired from discrete 74LS chips.

I am applying to MSc programmes in computer and embedded systems engineering.
