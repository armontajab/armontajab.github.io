Hi, I’m Alireza.

Electrical Engineering undergraduate — digital design & embedded systems

I’m an Electrical Engineering student at Shahid Beheshti University in Tehran, specialising in Digital Electronics, and an embedded hardware engineer at Tensor Intelligent Machines — a robotics company building AI-driven autonomous parcel-sorting robots.

My work sits where digital logic meets physical hardware: USB host firmware under FreeRTOS, bare-metal peripheral programming written against the registers rather than a library, SMD board design, and closed-loop systems that have to hold something steady in the real world.

What I do

Building systems that close the loop

Read a sensor, decide, drive an actuator, stay stable while doing it — that pattern runs through almost everything I build. A handheld terminal that enumerates a USB barcode scanner as a HID keyboard under FreeRTOS. A dual-loop STM32 controller regulating water level and temperature at once. A steel ball suspended in mid-air by a PID loop that uses a camera as its only position sensor.

I’m currently extending my focus toward FPGA design and hardware description languages, and applying to MSc programmes in Canada in computer and embedded systems engineering.
