About

A bit about me

Electrical Engineering undergraduate at Shahid Beheshti University and embedded hardware engineer at Tensor Intelligent Machines.

I selected the Digital Electronics specialisation in February 2026, after coursework in circuit analysis, electronics and digital systems made it clear that the part I cared about was how a design behaves once it leaves the schematic. Deriving a transfer function is satisfying; watching the same circuit hold a setpoint on a bench is what made me want to keep going.

Most of what I’ve built since has been systems that close a loop. Some of it is firmware written directly against timers, input capture and interrupts rather than vendor drivers, because I wanted to understand what the peripheral was actually doing. Some of it is hardware — routing my own board in Altium instead of reaching for an integrated driver. And some of it is old-fashioned discrete logic, cascading adders, multiplexers, decoders and shift registers into a working datapath.

Since September 2025 I have been working at Tensor Intelligent Machines, first as an engineering intern and since June 2026 as an embedded hardware engineer. Building things that ship to a customer rather than to a grader changed how I work: timeouts, failure modes and sourcing turned out to matter as much as whether the algorithm is correct.

I’m now applying to MSc programmes in Canada in computer and embedded systems engineering, where I’d like to work on digital design and hardware–software co-design under supervision.
