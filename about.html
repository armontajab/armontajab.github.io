About

Electrical Engineering undergraduate at Shahid Beheshti University and embedded hardware engineer at Tensor Intelligent Machines.

I chose the Digital Electronics specialisation in my third year, after coursework in circuit analysis, electronics and digital systems made it clear which part I cared about. Deriving a transfer function is satisfying. Watching the same circuit hold a setpoint on a bench is what made me want to keep going.

Most of what I have built since closes a loop. Some of it is firmware written directly against timers, input capture and interrupts rather than vendor drivers, because I wanted to know what the peripheral was actually doing. Some of it is hardware, routing my own boards in Altium instead of reaching for an integrated driver. And some of it is discrete logic, cascading adders, multiplexers, decoders and shift registers into a working datapath.

Since September 2025 I have been at Tensor Intelligent Machines, first as an engineering intern and since June 2026 as an embedded hardware engineer. Building things that ship to a customer rather than to a grader changed how I work. Timeouts, failure modes and part sourcing turned out to matter as much as whether the algorithm is correct.
