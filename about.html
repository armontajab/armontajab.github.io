As an Electrical Engineering student, I selected the Digital Electronics specialisation in February 2026, following coursework in circuit analysis, electronics, and digital systems that led me to focus more closely on how designs perform in practical settings. While deriving a transfer function is an important part of understanding a system, seeing the same circuit maintain a setpoint in practice strengthened my interest in digital and embedded systems.

Since then, I have built systems involving closed-loop operation, low-level firmware, hardware design, and discrete digital logic. Some involved firmware developed through direct programming of timers, input capture, and interrupts rather than relying on vendor drivers, allowing me to understand more closely how the underlying peripherals operate. Others involved hardware design, including PCB layout and routing in Altium. I have also worked with discrete digital logic, combining adders, multiplexers, decoders, and shift registers to build functional datapaths.

Since September 2025, I have been working at Tensor Intelligent Machines, first as an Engineering Intern and since June 2026 as an Embedded Hardware Engineer. Working on systems intended for real-world use rather than academic evaluation changed how I approach engineering. Timeout handling, understanding failure modes, and finding and adapting relevant libraries and sample code turned out to matter as much as the correctness of the algorithm.

These experiences have shaped my current interests in digital design, embedded systems, and hardware–software co-design.
